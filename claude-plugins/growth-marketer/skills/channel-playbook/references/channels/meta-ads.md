# Meta Ads Channel Reference

## When to Choose

Use Meta Ads when the service can be explained visually, has a broad or lookalike-ready audience, and needs paid social demand generation or conversion scaling across Facebook and Instagram. For B2C mobile apps and consumer subscriptions, start with conversion or app-promotion objectives rather than traffic-only campaigns when downstream action quality matters.

## Targeting

- Start from the ICP in `service-profile.json`: segment, pains, goals, language, and market.
- Keep early ad sets broad enough for delivery learning. Use geography, age, language, and only the highest-signal interest or behavior constraints.
- Use Custom Audiences for site visitors, app users, customer lists, and engagement audiences when enough first-party data exists.
- Treat Advantage+ audience as a way to extend beyond audience suggestions, not as a replacement for clear message-market fit.

## Budget and Bidding

- Pick the objective that matches the desired user action: Sales, Leads, or App promotion for conversion-oriented goals.
- For freemium or subscription apps, optimize toward the closest reliable high-quality event available: install first, then trial_start or purchase when volume supports it.
- Avoid optimizing for link clicks if the business goal is signup, purchase, or trial quality.
- Keep initial budgets stable long enough to compare creatives and audiences before reallocating.

## Creative Specs

- Test vertical short video, square product screenshot, UGC-style proof, and direct benefit-first static assets.
- Prepare placement-aware variants for Reels, Stories, Feed, and square placements.
- Align the first-screen landing or store promise with the ad promise.
- Include product shot, outcome, proof, and CTA variants instead of only brand-level awareness copy.

## Measurement KPIs

- Primary: cost per signup, cost per trial_start, cost per purchase, trial-to-paid rate.
- Secondary: thumbstop rate, CTR, landing conversion rate, frequency, creative fatigue.
- Compare each creative angle against downstream quality, not only cheap clicks.
