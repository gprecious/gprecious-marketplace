# SEO / Content Channel Reference

## When to Choose

Use SEO/content when users actively search for the problem, category, comparison, workflow, or implementation guidance. It is strongest for high-discovery-intent categories and for B2B or considered purchases where education precedes conversion.

## Targeting

- Segment search intent into information, comparison, purchase, problem-solving, and brand exploration.
- Match page type to intent: article, comparison page, template, checklist, landing page, or FAQ.
- Use ICP pains and vocabulary from `service-profile.json` as query language, but preserve accuracy and helpfulness.
- Internal links should move users to the next logical action without forcing every visitor into sales immediately.

## Budget and Bidding

- SEO uses content and technical effort, not media bids.
- Prioritize pages where the service can add original information, complete explanation, or strong practical utility.
- Build durable pages around category and pain terms before producing high-volume but weakly related content.
- For paid search plus SEO, use organic pages to learn message fit and paid campaigns to validate commercial terms faster.

## Creative Specs

- Titles should accurately summarize the page and match the query promise.
- Structure content with headings, internal links, clear answers, examples, and FAQ where useful.
- Add image alt text and structured data only when it describes real page content.
- CTA should match intent depth: template download, price page, demo, trial, diagnosis, or product comparison.

## Measurement KPIs

- Indexed pages, ranking movement, organic clicks, query CTR, and engaged sessions.
- Assisted conversions, email capture, demo requests, signup, or purchase from organic paths.
- Content refresh impact and internal-link assisted movement to product pages.
- Quality checks: originality, sourcing, helpfulness, and page experience.
