# Cognitive Psychology Technique Catalog

Use these techniques as hypothesis generators for copy, not as guaranteed conversion formulas. Every output must annotate technique IDs and source references, then define a measurable hypothesis.

## Technique IDs

### `reciprocity`

Give useful value before asking for a higher-friction action. Examples: checklist, audit snippet, benchmark, or template before demo/signup. Source: Cialdini, "Harnessing the Science of Persuasion" and Cialdini & Goldstein, "The Science and Practice of Persuasion":
- https://www.influenceatwork.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/Harvard-Business-Review-.pdf
- https://www2.psych.ubc.ca/~schaller/308Readings/Cialdini2002.pdf

### `micro-commitment`

Turn the first CTA into a small honest choice, such as "무료 진단 보기", "내 업종 선택하기", or "체크리스트 완료하기", before asking for demo, signup, or purchase. Source: Cialdini and Goldstein:
- https://www2.psych.ubc.ca/~schaller/308Readings/Cialdini2002.pdf

### `social-proof`

Reduce uncertainty with similar-user proof: role-specific testimonials, verified review counts, segment-matched customer counts, or sourced behavior data. Source: Cialdini HBR; FTC endorsement and fake-review guidance:
- https://www.influenceatwork.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/Harvard-Business-Review-.pdf
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
- https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials

### `authority`

Expose real expertise signals: named credential, benchmark source, audit count, certification, review source, or relevant customer category. Do not use empty "expert-approved" language. Source: Cialdini HBR:
- https://www.influenceatwork.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/Harvard-Business-Review-.pdf

### `loss-aversion`

Frame real cost, time, risk, or missed opportunity against a concrete reference point, then immediately give a credible mitigation path. Source: Kahneman and Tversky, "Prospect Theory":
- https://studylib.net/doc/28016460/kahneman-tversky-1979-prospect-theory

### `anchoring`

Set a truthful comparison point before the offer: existing workflow cost, benchmark, monthly vs annual price, time spent, or agency audit price. Source: Epley and Gilovich:
- https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01704.x

### `priming`

Use category, audience, or value cues before the main claim so the user interprets copy through the intended frame. Example: "For solo founders" before a productivity claim. Source: Pacheco Junior et al.:
- https://pepsic.bvsalud.org/scielo.php?lng=en&pid=S1808-42812015000100016&script=sci_abstract

### `scarcity`

Use only real scarcity: limited cohort seats, operational onboarding capacity, inventory, real deadline, or invite-only beta capacity. Source: Cialdini HBR; Przybylski et al. on FOMO:
- https://www.influenceatwork.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/Harvard-Business-Review-.pdf
- https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/2013_PrzybylskiMurayamaDeHaanGladwell_CIHB.pdf

### `visual-hierarchy`

For assets that include layout notes, keep the user scan path clear: headline, proof, CTA, and product cue should not compete. Color is a contrast/context tool, not a universal conversion rule. Source: NN/g and WCAG:
- https://media.nngroup.com/media/articles/attachments/Principles_Visual_Design-A4.pdf
- https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/

### `aida-pas-fab`

Use AIDA for colder attention flows, PAS for pain-aware traffic, and FAB for feature-to-benefit translation. Source:
- https://kopywriting.com/blogs/287-copywriting-formulas/

## Measurement Discipline

Each generated asset must include:
- hypothesis
- primary metric, such as CTR, signup start, activation, reply, or click
- guardrail metric, such as conversion quality, unsubscribe, spam complaint, or paid conversion
- one main variable changed per A/B pair

Sources:
- https://www.optimizely.com/optimization-glossary/ab-testing/
- https://www.optimizely.com/sample-size-calculator/
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10618-008-0114-1

## Out Of Bounds

Do not generate or recommend:
- fake scarcity, fake countdowns, "always ending today" deadlines, or invented waitlists
- deceptive urgency where no real deadline or capacity limit exists
- fake testimonials, fake reviews, AI-generated customer quotes, bought followers, or fabricated customer counts
- fabricated price anchors, unsupported ROI, invented savings, or unverifiable benchmarks
- fear-based manipulation that exaggerates harm beyond the evidence
- hidden commitments, misleading free-trial wording, or unclear cost escalation
