# ASO Channel Reference

## When to Choose

Use ASO for mobile apps where App Store or Google Play discovery and install conversion are material to growth. It is especially useful before paid app campaigns, because paid traffic quality is constrained by the store listing promise and screenshots.

## Targeting

- Map ICP pains and goals to store search phrases, category phrases, and screenshot promises.
- Use separate Apple Custom Product Pages for distinct paid audiences when the message differs.
- Localize metadata and screenshots for the service market from `channel_signals.market`.
- Avoid keyword stuffing; prioritize relevance and install confidence.

## Budget and Bidding

- ASO itself is not a bid channel, but paid UA should not scale before baseline listing CVR is reasonable.
- Use Product Page Optimization or store listing experiments before declaring a screenshot or icon winner.
- Paid app campaigns should send audience-specific ads to matching custom pages when possible.

## Creative Specs

- App name/title, subtitle or short description, keyword metadata, icon, screenshots, preview video, and full description must work together.
- First screenshots should show outcome and product state, not only feature inventory.
- Google Play short description must communicate the core value quickly and fit its 80-character constraint.
- Apple Custom Product Pages can vary screenshot, promotional text, and preview for audience-specific promises.

## Measurement KPIs

- Store listing impression-to-install conversion rate.
- Search ranking for priority category and brand terms.
- Paid traffic install CVR by custom page or listing variant.
- Downstream trial_start, purchase, retention, and review quality by listing experiment.
