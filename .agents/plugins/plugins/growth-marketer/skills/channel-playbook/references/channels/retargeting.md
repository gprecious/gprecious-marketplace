# Retargeting / Remarketing Channel Reference

## When to Choose

Use retargeting when the service already has meaningful site, app, CRM, store, video, or ad-engagement audiences. It is a conversion and objection-handling layer, not a substitute for cold acquisition.

## Targeting

- Build funnel-stage audiences: all visitors, product viewers, pricing viewers, add-to-cart no purchase, checkout abandoners, trial started no activation, activated no paid, past purchasers, high-LTV customers.
- Exclude users who already completed the acquisition goal from acquisition retargeting.
- Use customer lists or engaged audiences only with consent and platform policy compliance.
- Keep sensitive categories and personalized advertising restrictions in scope before writing audience logic.

## Budget and Bidding

- Size budgets to audience volume; small audiences saturate quickly.
- Bid more aggressively for high-intent windows such as cart abandonment or pricing-page views.
- Refresh creative and windows when frequency rises or CPA degrades.
- Separate cross-sell, onboarding, and winback from first-purchase retargeting.

## Creative Specs

- 1-3 days: reminder and core benefit.
- 4-7 days: proof, reviews, FAQ, or setup guidance.
- 8-14 days: comparison, objection handling, or stronger offer.
- 15-30 days: new angle, seasonal offer, cross-sell, or education.
- Dynamic product ads should use product ID, price, and viewed-item context when available.

## Measurement KPIs

- Audience size, match rate, frequency, CPA, conversion lag, and incremental lift where measurable.
- Funnel-stage conversion rate: cart-to-purchase, trial-to-activation, activation-to-paid.
- Exclusion quality: completed leads/purchasers should not keep receiving acquisition messages.
- Revenue per retargeted user and cross-sell/retention impact for existing customers.
