# Naver Search + GFA Channel Reference

## When to Choose

Use Naver when the service targets Korea, Korean-language buyers, local intent, shopping intent, app conversion tracking, or categories where Naver search and Naver-owned surfaces shape discovery. Use search ads for intent capture and GFA for audience/display expansion or retargeting.

## Targeting

- Search: split keywords by Korean problem phrase, category phrase, price phrase, and local phrase.
- Reflect Naver-native trust cues such as Naver Pay, reservation, Smart Store, place, reviews, and local distance when relevant.
- GFA: begin with interest, purchase-intent, demographic, advertiser-owned, or AI-recommended audiences.
- App campaigns need MAT/MMP conversion tracking before judging downstream quality.

## Budget and Bidding

- Search bidding should reflect keyword intent and landing relevance, not only rank.
- Improve material quality by matching ad copy to landing title, product name, and description.
- GFA can use CPM, CPC, or CPV depending on campaign goal.
- Conversion optimization should wait until enough event collection exists.

## Creative Specs

- Search copy should answer the exact Korean query and avoid vague brand slogans.
- Include region, price condition, booking, Pay, or store cues only when true.
- GFA creative should separate prospecting benefit angles from retargeting offer/proof angles.
- For shopping or local services, keep product/category imagery direct and easy to compare.

## Measurement KPIs

- Search: keyword-level CPA, CTR, landing conversion rate, ad rank quality signals, query coverage.
- GFA: CPM/CPC/CPV by goal, conversion CPA, audience-level event quality, creative fatigue.
- App: tracked install, in-app event conversion, MAT audience growth, cost per purchase or subscription.
